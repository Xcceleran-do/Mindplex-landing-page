# Mindplex Landing Page Redesign

## Executive read

The legacy landing page is not failing because it lacks polish. It is failing because it has no single job. It presents Mindplex as a magazine, social network, AI company, blockchain protocol, token economy, licensing business, and community initiative at the same level of importance. The result is a long page that asks visitors to decode the company before they can decide whether to enter it.

The redesigned beta product provides the answer. Mindplex is best understood as an editorial and community platform for people following AI, science, technology, and culture. Its strongest proposition is not "decentralized media." It is a place to find signal, discuss consequential ideas, and build visible credibility through contribution.

The standalone landing page should therefore function as a clear front door to the beta product, not a second content site and not a token whitepaper rendered as marketing.

## What is broken on the legacy page

### Positioning

- The hero leads with implementation language: "Blockchain-based Decentralized Media Network."
- The value proposition is a list of categories rather than a visitor outcome.
- Six different business models compete for attention.
- The primary call to action points to the magazine even though the copy describes a much broader platform.
- "Mindplexes" introduces a new undefined term at the conversion point.

### Content

- Long paragraphs repeat the same ideas with different buzzwords.
- Claims such as "Fast," "Reliable," "Next Gen," and "AI Solutions" are unsupported.
- Copy includes grammar and capitalization errors.
- The governance section exposes unfinished formula text and "T = XXX."
- "anyone can stake MPX and we compensate handsomely" is not credible institutional language.
- Team cards duplicate names and roles in the accessible tree.
- Some profile links are blank or contain malformed whitespace.

### Information architecture

- The top navigation gives equal weight to About, AI, Roadmap, Tokens, Blog, Team, Whitepaper, Contact, and Join.
- The page moves from hero to company description to six token cards to a generic community pitch, then back to AI and finally to a large team directory.
- The highest-value product routes in the beta, including Magazine, Podcast, News, Topics, Community, and Mindbytes, are absent from the landing page story.
- Tokenomics appears before visitors understand why the platform matters.

### Visual system

- The page uses the familiar AI template stack: dark navy, cyan-purple gradients, glass cards, glow effects, floating badges, and a generic 3D object.
- Every section is card-shaped, so hierarchy collapses.
- Equal card grids repeat without compositional variation.
- Decorative badges such as "Fast," "Reliable," and "Active" do not carry real state.
- The interface looks like a crypto landing-page kit rather than an editorial institution.

### Motion and performance

- The Spline hero adds network and rendering cost without explaining the product.
- Repeating pulse and bounce animations are not tied to hierarchy, feedback, or state.
- Animation is used as decoration while meaningful transitions and tactile states are inconsistent.
- The current hero depends on a remote 3D scene, which increases LCP risk and creates a fragile first impression.

### Accessibility and trust

- Several headings are duplicated.
- Decorative images carry content-like alternative text.
- The mobile navigation button lacks a clear accessible label and expanded state.
- Focus treatment is not systematized.
- Copy errors, unfinished mathematics, and malformed links create a larger trust problem than any visual defect.

## What the beta establishes

The beta product has a much stronger foundation:

- Near-black page surface: `#121317`
- Off-white foreground: `#edf0f3`
- Quiet gray hierarchy
- Mindplex mint primary around `#53c99a`
- A 65px navigation bar
- Tight, editorial display type with negative tracking
- Real story photography
- Twelve-pixel media and surface radii
- Clear product routes: Magazine, Podcast, News, Topics, Community, and Mindbytes
- A more focused voice: "AI, media, and community intelligence"

The landing page should inherit those tokens and that voice while using larger, more cinematic compositions.

## New positioning

### Core promise

**Make sense of what comes next.**

Mindplex brings independent media, community intelligence, and emerging AI tools together for people tracking the future.

### Audience

- Readers who want edited context instead of another undifferentiated feed
- Writers, researchers, artists, and technologists with something useful to contribute
- Builders and institutions exploring AI-assisted media and reputation systems

### Product story

1. Read deeply through magazine, podcast, and long-form work.
2. Track change through the newsroom, topics, and Mindbytes.
3. Contribute through discussion, original work, and a reputation-aware community.
4. Understand how AI and visible attribution support the editorial process.

## New page architecture

1. **Navigation:** product-first links and one clear entry CTA
2. **Hero:** a visitor outcome, one sentence of context, and a real editorial image
3. **Topic rail:** a single motion device that communicates subject breadth
4. **Positioning statement:** the publication-plus-community distinction
5. **Three entry modes:** Read, Track, Contribute
6. **Trust:** attribution, editorial judgment, and reputation
7. **AI in the workflow:** research and production support with human judgment visible
8. **Community:** a direct invitation to participate
9. **Destination index:** the beta's real product routes
10. **Final CTA and footer**

## Art direction

- **Theme:** dark only, matching the beta product
- **Accent:** Mindplex mint only
- **Type:** system grotesk with optical sizing, tight display tracking, and comfortable body leading
- **Imagery:** authentic Mindplex editorial photography and product imagery from the redesigned beta
- **Surfaces:** mostly open layout and hairline grouping; cards only where hierarchy requires containment
- **Radii:** 12px for media and contained surfaces; 10px for buttons and controls
- **Motion:** hero sequencing, one topic rail, subtle viewport reveals, and tactile press states
- **Avoid:** purple-blue glow, gradient text, glass-card grids, fake dashboards, token coins, 3D blobs, decorative status dots, generic superlatives, and perpetual motion without purpose

## Content rules

- Lead with what a visitor can do, not the underlying technology.
- Keep blockchain and token mechanics out of the primary conversion path.
- Describe AI as part of a workflow, not as a magical actor.
- Use concrete verbs: read, track, discuss, publish, contribute.
- Preserve the beta's product names and routes.
- Do not invent audience, growth, token, or engagement metrics.
- Keep the whitepaper available as a resource, not as primary navigation.

## Success criteria

- A visitor can explain Mindplex after reading the hero and first section.
- The page makes the redesigned beta feel like the obvious next click.
- The visual language is recognizably related to the beta without duplicating its homepage.
- The page contains no unfinished roadmap or token copy.
- Navigation and CTAs are usable with keyboard, pointer, and touch.
- The hero has no remote runtime dependency.
- Editorial imagery is sourced from the beta product, optimized appropriately, and reserves layout space.
- All movement respects reduced-motion preferences.
